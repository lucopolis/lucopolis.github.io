It is with great joy that we open registration for our Apometry Training Program — a journey of learning, practice, and expansion of consciousness.

Throughout the course, we will explore topics such as consciousness, subtle bodies, the energetic field, and processes that support the spiritual and emotional balance of the Being.

Apometry is a therapeutic and spiritual technique developed in Brazil. It works through the expansion of consciousness and energetic practices across different levels of the Being. Its study brings together knowledge related to spirituality, energy, mediumship, emotions, unconscious patterns, and the multidimensional nature of the human experience.

This training was created for those who wish to deepen their energetic and spiritual understanding through a practical, conscious, universalist, and non-dogmatic approach.

The program includes 10 in-person sessions with theoretical content, practical activities, exercises, and experiences designed to develop energetic perception. Each session has been thoughtfully created to offer a gradual, meaningful, and transformative learning journey.
